# About Plenty

Plenty is Carmine Paolino's company for AI-native work tools, RubyLLM, ArchSpec, AI and Ruby consulting, and hands-on workshops.

The work centers on practical software products and open source tools, including [Chat with Work](https://chatwithwork.com), [RubyLLM](https://rubyllm.com), [ArchSpec](https://archspecrb.dev), [RubyLLM Workshops](https://github.com/crmne/ruby_llm-workshop), [Cluster Headache Tracker](https://clusterheadachetracker.com), [hyprmoncfg](https://hyprmoncfg.dev), [Jekyll VitePress](https://jekyll-vitepress.dev), and [Kamal Backup](https://kamal-backup.dev).

Carmine Paolino leads Plenty and works with founders and teams on product direction, implementation strategy, Ruby, and AI application development. You can find Carmine at [paolino.me](https://paolino.me), on the [services page](https://paolino.me/services/), and at [Ruby Events](https://www.rubyevents.org/profiles/crmne).
